# Aura Demo Video Script

**Target length:** 2:30–2:45
**Format:** Screen recording + face-cam PiP bookends
**Audio:** Voiceover + live Aura voice + subtle ambient electronic
**Captions:** Burned-in throughout (85% of social plays start muted)
**Visual cursor indicator:** Ripple animation on every click Aura makes (critical for muted viewing)

---

## PRE-PRODUCTION NOTES

- Record on a clean macOS desktop, minimal wallpaper
- Have Safari, Notes, Mail, and a form-heavy website loaded and ready
- Stage the desktop for the "personality moment" — 15-20 tabs open, a folder named something amusing, give Aura ammunition to be funny
- Menu bar should show the Aura icon clearly
- Use headphones during recording (keeps Aura's voice clean in capture)
- Screen record at native resolution, crop to 16:9 in post
- Record face-cam separately, composite as PiP
- **Record every demo 5-8 times.** Aura's responses vary in length, tone, and quality — you need a deep bench of takes to edit from
- Plan a 9:16 vertical crop for TikTok/Reels/Shorts — frame demos so the key action stays center-screen

---

## THE SCRIPT

### COLD OPEN — The impossible thing [0:00–0:22]

> This is the moment 80% of viewers decide to stay or leave. Do NOT rush it. Let the audience sit with the reality that nobody is touching the mouse.

**[SCREEN: Notes app is open, empty. Desktop is visible. The cursor is still.]**

**[AUDIO: Your voice, casual, like you're talking to someone in the room]**
> "Hey Aura — I'm going to Tokyo next month. Can you make me a packing list?"

**[2-second pause. Then Aura's voice responds — warm, conversational. As it talks, the cursor moves to the Notes app on its own. Starts typing. Lines appear one by one: clothes, toiletries, adapters, travel docs. Clean, fast, visible.]**

**[CAPTION at 0:03, burned in: "I'm not touching the keyboard or mouse."]**

**[Let this run in REAL TIME. No speed ramp. The unedited reality of a cursor moving autonomously is the entire pitch. Hold until the list is 6-8 items.]**

**[CAPTION at 0:18: "That's a native macOS app — not a browser extension."]**

**Max Aura response time: 4 seconds of talking before it starts acting. If a take has Aura monologuing for 10+ seconds before moving the cursor, discard it.**

---

### THE BUILDER [0:22–0:40]

**[CUT TO: Face-cam. You, at your desk. Natural lighting. Casual.]**

**[VOICEOVER (you, to camera):]**
> "I'm Abdullahi. I built Aura — a native macOS app that sees your screen, hears your voice, and controls your computer."

**[Beat.]**

> "This isn't a chatbot in a browser. It sees the actual pixels on your screen and controls the actual mouse on your desk."

**[While you talk, QUICK B-ROLL CUTS (1-2 seconds each): the Aura menu bar icon pulsing green, the popover UI showing a conversation, a terminal with `cargo build` output, a glimpse of the Cargo.toml workspace members.]**

---

### DEMO 1 — Form filling [0:40–1:18]

**[SCREEN: A registration or contact form — 5-6 visible fields, clearly empty. Show the URL bar so it's clear this is a real website.]**

**[TIMER OVERLAY: Small, top-right corner. Starts counting when you speak.]**

**[Your voice, live:]**
> "Aura, fill this out for me. You know my info."

**[SCREEN: Aura responds briefly (MAX 5 seconds of speech — "On it, let me read through this"). Then the cursor moves. Clicks first field. Types first name. Tabs to next field. Last name. Email. Phone. It reads the form and fills it methodically.]**

**[Let the first 2-3 fields run in real time so it's clearly real. Then SPEED RAMP to 2x for the middle fields (no more than 8 seconds at 2x). Return to real-time for the scroll down + submit button click.]**

**[On the submit click: ripple animation on cursor, subtle click sound, TIMER STOPS.]**

**[CAPTION on submit: "42 seconds from voice to submitted form"]** *(adjust to actual time)*

**[Aura, after submitting (MAX 3 seconds): something like "Done, you're all set."]**

---

### THE ARCHITECTURE BEAT [1:18–1:28]

**[SCREEN: Clean animated graphic — three words appearing one by one with simple icons. Each word has a subtitle line visible ON SCREEN (critical for muted viewers):]**

```
SEES    — screen capture at 2 FPS
HEARS   — real-time native audio
ACTS    — mouse, keyboard, AppleScript
```

**[VOICEOVER (fast, punchy, parallel structure):]**
> "It sees your screen. It hears your voice. It acts through the same controls you use. 20,000 lines of Rust. One menu bar icon."

**[10 seconds max. Don't linger.]**

---

### DEMO 2 — The "wow" moment [1:28–2:00]

> This is the most impressive thing Aura can do. Let Aura's voice carry this scene. You stay quiet after the initial prompt.

**[SCREEN: An invoice, receipt, or article is open in Safari — something with real content.]**

**[Your voice:]**
> "Aura, can you read what's on my screen and draft a quick email about it to Sarah?"

**[SCREEN: Aura responds briefly (MAX 5 seconds) — acknowledges the task. Then it reads the screen content. Opens Mail. The compose window appears. Aura types a subject line and body that references the actual content on screen. This is the proof that it SEES and UNDERSTANDS, not just clicks.]**

**[KEY: Let Aura talk while it works — narrating what it's doing. This is the product. The voice + autonomous action happening simultaneously is what no other tool does. DON'T narrate over it.]**

**[If Aura summarizes the content accurately and writes a coherent email, hold on the finished draft for 3 seconds. Let the audience read it.]**

**Max Aura response/narration: 20 seconds total across this entire demo. If a take runs over 30 seconds, discard it.**

---

### THE PERSONALITY MOMENT [2:00–2:12]

**[SCREEN: Desktop visible with staged mess — many tabs, cluttered dock, the amusing folder name.]**

**[Your voice:]**
> "Aura, be honest — how messy is my desktop?"

**[Aura responds — roasts your tabs, comments on the chaos, says something with personality.]**

**[CRITICAL FOR MUTED VIEWERS: Caption Aura's entire response. And show face-cam PiP in this section so viewers see you laughing/reacting. Without audio, the visual of you laughing at your computer's response is what sells this moment.]**

**Max Aura response: 8 seconds. If it gives a bland "I see several windows open" answer, re-prompt or use a different take.**

---

### THE CLOSE [2:12–2:35]

**[CUT TO: Face-cam.]**

**[VOICEOVER:]**
> "I spent six months fighting WebSocket frames and macOS accessibility APIs so I could say 'fill this out' and watch it happen."

**[Beat.]**

> "It's open source. 20,000 lines of Rust, 10 crates, zero Electron."

**[CUT TO: Screen. One final, quick interaction — you say something simple, Aura does it instantly. 3 seconds. The effortless punctuation mark.]**

> "If you want a computer that listens — link's in the description. Star the repo."

**[SCREEN: GitHub repo URL, clean and centered. Hold for 3 seconds.]**

**[Fade to black.]**

## POST-PRODUCTION

### Music
- Subtle electronic ambient (Tycho, Boards of Canada aesthetic)
- Barely noticeable during demos — Aura's voice is the star
- **Dip to silence** before each "wow" moment: the form submit, the email draft reveal, the desktop roast
- Fade out under final face-cam

### Editing
- **Speed ramps:** 2x ONLY through repetitive mid-form typing. Everything else real-time.
- **Cursor ripple:** Add a subtle expanding circle animation at cursor position for every click. This is non-negotiable — it makes clicks legible muted.
- **Transitions:** Simple hard cuts. No zooms, spins, or flashy transitions.
- **Captions:** White text, semi-transparent dark pill background, bottom center. Always on. Caption your voice AND Aura's voice.
- **Face-cam PiP:** Bottom-left, circular crop, ~15% of frame. Only during builder intro (0:22–0:40), personality moment (2:00–2:12), and close (2:12–2:35).
- **Timer overlay:** Small, unobtrusive, top-right. Only during Demo 1. Shows elapsed seconds.

### Thumbnail (choose one)
- **Option A:** Split screen — left half shows your hand on a mouse, right half shows the cursor moving with no hand. Text: "I talk. It clicks."
- **Option B:** Screen recording frame with Aura mid-action, your face-cam PiP in corner reacting. Text: "20,000 lines of Rust for this."
- **Option C:** Close-up of the menu bar icon with a speech bubble. Text: "My Mac does what I say."

### Sound design
- Click sound effect on every Aura click (subtle, satisfying)
- Soft keyboard sounds when Aura types (barely audible, adds texture)
- One transition whoosh between sections
- Aura's voice = loudest element in the mix

## RECORDING CHECKLIST

Before you hit record:

- [ ] Clean desktop with staged "messy" elements for personality moment
- [ ] Do Not Disturb ON — no notification interruptions
- [ ] Menu bar visible, Aura icon showing
- [ ] OBS or native screen recording ready
- [ ] Face-cam framed, lit, focused
- [ ] Headphones on
- [ ] Test each demo scenario 2-3 times — note which prompts get the best Aura responses
- [ ] Browser history/bookmarks/tabs presentable
- [ ] Font size readable on mobile (bump if needed)
- [ ] Form website loaded and tested
- [ ] Invoice/receipt/article loaded in Safari for Demo 2
- [ ] Notes app open and empty for cold open
- [ ] Mail app logged in and ready

## DEALING WITH FAILURES

Aura won't be perfect on every take. That's fine.

**Record 5-8 takes of each demo.** Use the best one. This isn't a live performance.

**If a demo fails during what would otherwise be a great take:** Keep rolling. Say "Not quite — let me try that again." A recovery moment builds trust with technical audiences. A flawless demo looks rehearsed. One stumble and recovery looks real.

**Do NOT fake any interaction.** Screen recordings are trivially verifiable. One fake demo will destroy credibility with the developer audience permanently.

**Have backup prompts ready:**
- Cold open backup: "Aura, what should I pack for a week in Tokyo?"
- Demo 1 backup: "Can you fill out my name and email on this form?"
- Demo 2 backup: "Summarize what's on my screen and write it up in Notes"
- Personality backup: "Rate my desktop setup, 1 to 10"
